Support

Getting started with EvenFold for Mac, iPhone, and iPad.

1 · Connect your model (BYOK)

Follow the first-issue guide, or open Settings → Model. Pick a provider, paste your API key, load and choose a model, then click Connect. EvenFold verifies the model before saving your key to the system keychain, and asks for your consent before sending selected content to your chosen provider. Signing in is optional.

2 · Add sources (optional)

Connect GitHub in Settings—or on Mac, choose Apple Mail accounts and local folders—and pick which repositories and mailboxes to follow.

3 · Read

A fresh edition is written at 06:00 and 18:00 while the app is open, or anytime you choose Refresh. Click or tap any story to read in full, leave feedback, or ask the assistant.

Sharing between devices

In Settings, set the storage location to iCloud Drive on each device. Model keys and source connections are set up separately on each device.

If an edition fails

Your previous edition remains readable. Check your internet connection and verify that your provider API key is active, then choose Refresh.

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel EvenFold Pro anytime in your Apple ID Account Settings, or use Restore Purchases in Settings. See our Terms of Use for billing and renewal details.

帮助与支持

在 Mac、iPhone 与 iPad 上开始使用晨昏报。

一 · 连接模型（BYOK）

按创刊号引导操作，或打开「设置 → 模型」：选择服务商、填写 API 密钥、加载并选定模型后点击「连接」。晨昏报会先验证模型可用，再将密钥存入系统钥匙串，并在发送选定内容给第三方服务商前征求你的明确同意。账号登录为可选项。

二 · 添加来源（可选）

在设置中连接 GitHub，在 Mac 上也可以选取 Apple 邮件账户与本地文件夹，挑选需要纳入的仓库和邮箱。

三 · 阅读

App 打开时，每天 06:00 和 18:00 会各写一期；你也可以随时点击「刷新」。点开任意报道即可阅读全文、留下反馈，或请助手帮忙。

在设备之间共享

在各台设备的设置中将存储位置设为 iCloud Drive 即可同步报纸；模型密钥和来源连接需在每台设备上分别配置。

生成失败时

上一期报纸会继续保留可读。请检查网络连接并确认服务商 API 密钥有效，再点击「刷新」。

订阅与管理

你可以随时在 Apple ID 账户设置中管理或取消 EvenFold Pro 订阅，或在应用设置中「恢复购买」。计费与自动续订详情见使用条款。
